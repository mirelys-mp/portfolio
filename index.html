I am an undergraduate pursuing a B.S. in Design at the Stanford University School of Engineering and the Hasso Plattner Institute of Design, with a minor in Modern Languages (Spanish and Portuguese) and interdisciplinary honors in Education through the Graduate School of Education. I am a research assistant in the Designing Education Lab and the Ideal Research Lab at Stanford, where my work focuses on accessibility in engineering education for blind and low-vision learners.

✿ RESEARCH

Designing Education Lab: IncluSim Accessible Circuit Simulator

Problem: Circuit simulators are visual, which shuts out blind and low-vision (BLV) engineering students.
My role: designer / researcher
Process: Co-designed tactile hardware, Braille embossing, and digital interfaces with the BLV community.
Result: The first accessible electronics circuit simulator of its kind, used in Stanford’s ENGR 40M. CHI 2025 Honorable Mention. Four co-authored peer-reviewed papers.

EduNLP Lab

Video annotator and student researcher. Coding gaze, gesture, and communication in multilingual student videos during collaborative math.
